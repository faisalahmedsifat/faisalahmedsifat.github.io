Software engineer and applied ML researcher based in Dhaka. My work lives at the boundary between distributed systems and machine learning research — I build infrastructure and study the theoretical problems that infrastructure exposes.

I've designed fault-tolerant execution engines, event-driven microservice architectures, and sandboxed multi-tenant compute pipelines. In parallel, I've published four peer-reviewed papers on 3D computer vision and medical AI in collaboration with the University of Adelaide and Flinders University.

I'm drawn to the problems that emerge when you try to run ML at the edges of a network — where bandwidth, compute, and reliability are all constrained simultaneously. That's where systems engineering and learning theory collide.
